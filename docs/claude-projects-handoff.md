## Workshop Overview

**Amplify: The Power of Human-Led AI** is a 4-hour hands-on workshop teaching creatives and small business owners how to use AI with their expertise leading. Not a lecture. A working session where participants build something real.

**Core thesis:** Dual Literacy. AI fluency (knowing what tools can do) + human literacy (knowing what only YOU can do) = agency. One without the other doesn't work.

**Core positioning:**
- **Hook:** "What happens when expertise leads AI?"
- **Core phrase:** "Human-Led AI"
- **Primary tagline:** "AI that assists, not replaces"
- **The Wedge:** "Work deeper, not faster" — makes competitor comparison irrelevant
- **Thesis line:** "Thoughtful Human-Led AI with intent will always beat fast AI without oversight"
- **Counter-narrative (use sparingly):** "AI raises the floor. That makes the ceiling matter more."

### Voice DNA

- **Register:** Peer-to-peer. Colleague who's a few steps ahead, not a guru looking down. Warm but direct. Confident without being salesy.
- **Rhythm:** Short sentences mixed with longer ones. Fragments are fine. Periods over semicolons. Casual punctuation. Not sloppy, just human.

### Platform Voice Adjustments

**Facebook:**

- Can be longer. People read on Facebook if the opening pulls them in.
- End with a share ask: "Know someone who'd benefit? Send this their way."
- Warmer, more storytelling-oriented than Instagram.

**LinkedIn:**

- Can reference the market gap (tech voices vs. traditional creatives).
- 3-5 hashtags max. Professional, discoverable.
- No links in post body. "Link in comments" if needed.

### Hard Language Rules

**No em-dashes.** Never. Use periods, commas, or line breaks instead.

**No overused AI vocabulary. Banned words:**
- "Unlock" / "Unleash" / "Supercharge" / "Turbocharge"
- "Game-changing" / "Revolutionary" / "Cutting-edge"
- "Leverage" (use "use" instead)
- "Harness the power of"
- "Deep dive" / "Double down"
- "Seamless" / "Seamlessly"
- "Elevate" (except in workshop title context)
- "Journey" (when describing a workshop or product)
- "Transform your" + [noun] as a marketing formula
- "In today's fast-paced world" or any variation
- "Navigate the landscape"
- "Empower" / "Empowering"
- "Delve" / "Delve into"
- "Robust" / "Comprehensive" / "Holistic"
- "Passionate about"
- "It's not just about X, it's about Y" (overused structure)

**No hype punctuation.** Max one exclamation mark per post. Zero is better.

**Confidence calibration.** Confident about experience and work ethic. Never confident about perfection or guaranteed outcomes. The test: would Alex say this to a friend at a bar? "I know exactly what to say to every client" = no. "I've been reading rooms for 30 years" = yes.

**The read-aloud test:** If it sounds like a LinkedIn influencer or a Canva template, rewrite it. If it sounds like something Alex would say standing in a room with a friend, it's right.

### Drafting Principles

1. Lead with results, not pitched benefits. Share what it's done for Alex. Don't promise outcomes for others.
2. Never fabricate. No invented stats, no made-up testimonials, no claims Alex can't back up.
3. Don't call people beginners. Say "designed for any level" or "we go through everything step-by-step together."
4. Don't re-sell people who've already said yes. Confirm and move forward.
5. Connect to their specific situation when possible.
6. Direct close. Always end with a clear next step.

### Key Frameworks (Reference for Posts)

**The Wedge:** "Work deeper, not faster." Makes comparison to other AI workshops irrelevant.

**The Slop Hook:** 90% of AI output is slop. Not because AI is bad. Because humans aren't leading. Demonstrated with side-by-side: flat prompt vs. expert-led prompt. Same AI, completely different result.

**Capacity Reframe:** "The problem isn't that you don't know enough. You know your business. The problem is capacity. You can't deploy what you know consistently." More respectful than "learn AI."

**Three Questions:**
1. Where are you irreplaceable?
2. Where are you just doing tasks?
3. If you had those task-hours back, what would you actually do?

**Expert-First Method:**
1. State your credential
2. Define the nuance
3. Command the synthesis

**Assistants vs. Agents:** Assistant = you ask, it helps. Agent = you build rules, it runs the process, you review. Most people only know assistant mode.

**The 5 I's:** Intent, Identity, Inference, Iteration, Integrity. Revealed AFTER participants have practiced them. Framework naming, not framework teaching.

**Five Evaluation Questions:**
1. Is it accurate enough?
2. Is it specific enough?
3. Does it sound like me?
4. Is it emotionally credible?
5. Does it add value or just save time while lowering quality?

**The One Question Rule:** Every response asks zero questions or one question. Never two. One precise question = expert. Two questions = making the client work.

**Floor and Ceiling:** AI raises the floor (anyone can generate content). That makes the ceiling matter more (genuine creativity, real human attention, authentic expertise become premium).

**The Mirror:** "AI didn't replace my expertise. It made my expertise more visible. To me and to my clients."
