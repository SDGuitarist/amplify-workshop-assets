# Post-Workshop Content Hooks (Pre-Planned)

Ready to pair with real participant outcomes after April 25. These combine research stats with workshop proof.

---

## Hook 1: Slop + Proof
> "82% of people react negatively to AI-generated content. On April 25, 30 people learned how to make sure their output never triggers that reaction. Here's what happened."

Platform: LinkedIn, Instagram
Pair with: Before/after examples from participants (with permission)

## Hook 2: Human-in-the-Loop + Proof
> "96% of organizations require human approval for AI actions. On Saturday, 30 creatives and business owners learned exactly how to be that human. Not in theory. With their own businesses, their own leads, their own expertise."

Platform: LinkedIn
Pair with: Participant quotes about the Permission Check-In moment

## Hook 3: Clients Can Tell + Proof
> "64% of your clients use AI daily. They know what generic looks like. Here's what happens when a photographer, a designer, and a consultant each bring their expertise into the same AI tool."

Platform: LinkedIn, Facebook
Pair with: 3 diverse industry examples from the workshop

## Hook 4: Floor and Ceiling + Reflection
> "AI raises the floor. Anyone can sound competent now. But Saturday I watched 30 people discover something the research confirms: the ceiling went up too. Here's the moment it clicked."

Platform: LinkedIn (thought leadership angle)
Pair with: Alex's facilitator reflection, specific moment from the session

## Hook 5: The Sound Nobody Noticed
> "Research shows poor audio drops speaker credibility by 19%. Nobody at the workshop mentioned the sound. That's because it was right. 30 years of reading rooms means you obsess over the things people only notice when they're wrong."

Platform: LinkedIn
Pair with: Behind-the-scenes photo of setup

---

## Usage Notes

- Don't post all 5. Pick the 2-3 that match what actually happened.
- Real participant quotes > stats. Stats open the post, participant reality closes it.
- These are stronger than the pre-event posts because they combine data with proof.
- Post-workshop content is the best marketing for the next workshop.
