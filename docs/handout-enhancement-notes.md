# Handout Enhancement Notes

When building the one-page take-home (5 I's, Expert-First, Five Questions, One Question Rule), consider adding a small "Why This Matters" sidebar with 2-3 key stats from the 2026 research.

## Suggested Stats for the Handout

Pick 2-3 max. The handout is a reference tool, not a research paper.

- **82%** of people react negatively to generic AI output. Your clients can feel the difference between real and generated.
- **96%** of organizations require human approval for AI. Being the expert in the loop is the standard, not the exception.
- **64%** of consumers use AI daily. They know what generic looks like. Your expertise in the prompt is what makes it stand out.

## Placement

A small box or sidebar on the back of the one-pager. Light gray background, 2-3 bullet points. Header: "The research behind the framework" or simply "Why this works."

Keep it subordinate to the framework itself. The 5 I's and Expert-First method are the deliverable. The stats are supporting context.
