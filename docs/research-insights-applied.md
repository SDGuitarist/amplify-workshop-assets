# Research Insights Applied to Amplify Workshop

**Sources:**
1. "Engineering a Sold-Out Event" — system viability, compound demand, structural vulnerabilities (2026)
2. "Enterprise Event Management in the Age of AI" — strategic analysis (2026)

**Date:** 2026-03-16

---

## Core Thesis (Both Reports Converge)

In a market saturated with AI tools and AI-generated noise, the winning strategy is human intentionality powered by AI — not AI replacing humans. Amplify's value proposition ("Human-Led AI") is the exact antidote both reports identify as the market gap.

---

## Applicable Insights

### 1. Authenticity Beats Polish — "Slop" Detection is Real

- 64% of consumers use AI daily and can instantly recognize AI-generated content
- 82% negative sentiment toward "AI slop" (mentions up 200% year-over-year)
- Audiences have developed "algorithmic literacy" — they know what fake looks like
- **Amplify application:** Keep LinkedIn posts in Alex's real voice. Use AI for structure, never for the narrative. The operator-recap format (specific system breakdowns, real numbers) is unfakeable and high-trust.

### 2. Value Transparency > Artificial Scarcity

- 73% of buyers value price transparency above all other factors
- 25% will pay more if cost drivers are clearly explained
- Steep "price cliff" jumps paralyze buyers; value-based framing converts
- **Amplify application:** The $100 early bird / $150 regular spread is gentle enough. But messaging should frame price as investment ("here's what $100 gets you that would take months alone") rather than countdown urgency ("early bird ends March 28").

### 3. Audio Fidelity = Credibility (Alex's Home Turf)

- Poor audio drops perceived speaker credibility by 19%
- Information retention drops 30% with low-quality sound — even when content is identical
- Audio quality diverts up to 40% of listener's mental resources to processing instead of learning
- **Amplify application:** Test Expressive Arts room acoustics before the event. Bring personal audio setup if needed. This is a genuine competitive advantage — most workshop facilitators don't think about sound at all. Consider mentioning this in marketing as a differentiator.

### 4. The Physical Experience is the Ultimate Anti-AI Product

- Digital AI fatigue has elevated the intrinsic value of in-person events
- Attendees seek physical gatherings to escape synthetic environments
- Messaging emphasizing exclusivity, micro-events, and genuine human connection outperforms generic AI-scripted value propositions
- **Amplify application:** 30 seats IS the feature, not a limitation. "A room small enough that everyone gets hands-on time" is premium positioning. Emphasize: real person, real room, real systems, real business — the thing online courses can't be.

### 5. Human-in-the-Loop is the Only Trusted AI Pattern

- Only 4% of enterprises allow AI agents to act without human approval
- 96% require human oversight — this isn't a limitation, it's the standard
- Trust in AI is earned incrementally through a "Graduated Trust Model"
- **Amplify application:** This validates the entire workshop thesis. Use in content: "96% of organizations still require human approval for AI actions. That's not a bug — that's the design." The workshop teaches exactly this — how to be the human in the loop effectively.

### 6. Real People Share > Brand Accounts

- Employee/advocate shares drive 30% more engagement and 2x click-through vs corporate channels
- Only 3% of people share corporate content, but those shares massively outperform
- **Amplify application:** Make it easy for attendees to share during and after the workshop. Give them a shareable moment (screenshot of their first automation, photo with their creation). Early attendees like Lynn become organic amplifiers if the experience is genuinely good.

### 7. Advanced Behavioral Signals Over Vanity Metrics

- LinkedIn algorithm in 2026 rewards: dwell time, thread depth (nested replies), return engagement
- Generic promotional posts don't trigger deep engagement
- Content must be "provocative, highly opinionated, or deeply educational" to perform
- **Amplify application:** Keep writing posts that teach something specific and end with a real question. The operator-recap format naturally generates longer reads and real comments. "AI workshop coming soon!" posts will be invisible.

## Not Applicable to Amplify (Noted for Reference)

- Enterprise procurement/RFP automation — audience is creatives and small business owners
- Biometric check-in and privacy compliance — 30-person workshop, not a conference
- Data warehouses, composable tech stacks, iPaaS — markdown files and cron jobs are right-sized
- Predictive analytics bias in programming — manual curation at this scale
- CAC compression math — marketing cost is time + LinkedIn organic reach
- Agentic AI production stack architecture — too technical for target audience
