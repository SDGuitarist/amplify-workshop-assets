# Workshop Segment: Making AI Sound Like You

**Where this fits:** Phase 5 (EXPERIENCE), after "Your Lead, Your Expertise" exercise (12:30-12:45), before the Five Evaluation Questions. Alternatively, can be woven into Phase 3 (ORIENT) as an extension of Expert-First prompting.

**Duration:** 10-12 minutes (can compress to 7 if time is tight)

**Why add this:** Evaluation Question #3 ("Does it sound like me?") currently has no teaching behind it. Participants get the question but not the method. This segment gives them practical tools to answer it. It also directly supports the Dual Literacy thesis: your voice IS the human literacy. If AI flattens it, you lost the whole point.

---

## The Setup (2 min)

After participants have generated their Expert-First outputs, pause before the evaluation step.

> "Before we evaluate those outputs, I want to show you something. Pull up what AI just wrote for you. Read the first two sentences out loud. Quietly, to yourself."

Pause. Let them read.

> "Now ask yourself one question: would you actually send that? Not 'is it accurate.' Not 'is it useful.' Would you put your name on it and feel good about it?"

Pause again.

> "If something felt off, even if you can't name why, that instinct is correct. And I'm going to show you exactly what's happening."

---

## The AI Tells Demo (3 min)

Show two versions of the same lead response on screen. One is raw AI output. One has been voice-matched.

**Raw AI output (project on screen):**

> "Thank you for reaching out! I'd be happy to discuss how we can make your event truly special. With over a decade of experience in the San Diego entertainment landscape, we pride ourselves on delivering seamless, tailored performances that elevate any occasion. Whether you're planning an intimate gathering or a grand celebration, our comprehensive approach ensures every detail is meticulously handled. Let's dive into how we can bring your vision to life!"

Ask the room: "What's wrong with this?" Collect answers. They'll say "generic," "sounds like a bot," "too salesy."

Then name the specific tells:
- "I'd be happy to" (Claude's signature opening)
- "entertainment landscape" (AI word: landscape)
- "seamless, tailored performances" (AI words: seamless, tailored)
- "elevate any occasion" (AI word: elevate)
- "comprehensive approach" (AI word: comprehensive)
- "meticulously handled" (AI word: meticulously)
- "Let's dive into" (AI phrase)
- One exclamation mark per sentence (hype punctuation)
- Every sentence is roughly the same length (no variation)
- Zero personal detail. Zero opinion. Zero risk.

**Voice-matched version:**

> "Hey, thanks for reaching out about the wedding. Quick question before I put anything together. Is this ceremony, cocktail hour, reception, or a combination? That changes the approach completely. For context, I've done about 200 weddings at venues around San Diego. Most of my Latin jazz work is cocktail hour and reception, but I've put together some great ceremony sets too. Once I know the shape of the evening I can send you options and pricing that actually fit."

Name what changed:
- Starts with a specific question (the One Question Rule)
- References actual experience (200 weddings, not "over a decade")
- Names the genre and venue context (Latin jazz, San Diego)
- Asks before proposing (expert move, not sales move)
- Sentence lengths vary: 7 words, then 10, then 25, then 18
- Sounds like a person. You can hear the voice.

---

## The Voice Training Method (5 min, hands-on)

> "Here's how you make AI sound like you. It takes about five minutes and it changes everything."

Walk participants through three steps. They do this live on their laptops.

### Step 1: Feed Your Voice (2 min)

> "Find two things you've written that sound like you. An email to a client. A text to a colleague. A social media post. Something where you were being yourself, not performing. Copy it into Claude."

> "Now type this: 'Analyze my writing voice. Identify my tone, sentence length patterns, words I use often, and words I avoid. Be specific.'"

Let them run it. Give them 90 seconds to read the result.

### Step 2: Set Your Boundaries (2 min)

> "Now tell the AI what you hate. Everyone has words that make them cringe. 'Leverage.' 'Synergy.' 'Unlock.' Whatever makes you want to close the laptop. Type those in."

> "Add this line: 'Never use these words or phrases in anything you write for me: [their list].'"

> "And add: 'Match my sentence length patterns. Vary short and long. No uniform rhythm.'"

### Step 3: Test It (1 min)

> "Now rerun the same prompt you used in the Expert-First exercise. Same request, same context. But this time, with your voice profile loaded."

> "Compare the two outputs. The difference is the difference between AI writing about your business and AI writing as your business."

---

## The Bridge to Evaluation (1 min)

> "This is why Question 3 matters. 'Does it sound like me?' isn't a vague feeling check. It's a technical question. Does the output match your sentence patterns? Does it use your vocabulary? Does it avoid words you'd never say? If you trained the AI on your voice and the output still fails Question 3, the problem is in the prompt, not the tool. Fix the input."

Transition directly into the Five Evaluation Questions.

## Key Stats to Drop (use 1-2, not all)

- "Slop" was Merriam-Webster's 2025 Word of the Year. It means AI-generated content with no point of view.
- AI uses em dashes at 3x the rate of human writers.
- GPTZero found the word "delve" appears 200x more often in AI text than human text.
- Suspected AI content reduces reader trust by nearly 50%.
- 55% of people trust brands with human-generated content more. For Gen Z and Millennials, it's 66%.
- AI-generated articles now make up more than half of all English-language content on the web.

Pick the one that lands hardest for this room. For creatives: the trust stat. For consultants: the "slop" definition. For everyone: the em dash stat gets a laugh.

## Connection to Workshop Themes

- **Dual Literacy:** Your voice IS the human literacy half. If AI flattens your voice, you've lost the point. Voice training is how you protect Identity (the second I).
- **The Slop Hook:** The opening demo (flat vs. expert-led) shows the content gap. This segment shows the voice gap. Same principle, different dimension.
- **Expert-First Prompting:** Voice training is the natural extension. Expert-First gets the content right. Voice training gets the delivery right.
- **The 5 I's:** This segment directly serves Identity ("your standards, your point of view") and Iteration ("revise the prompt, not the output").
- **Floor and Ceiling:** When everyone can generate content, voice is the ceiling. Generic AI output is the floor. Voice training is how you stay above it.
