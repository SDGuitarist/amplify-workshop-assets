## Content Rules

1. No fabricated engagement metrics. Don't say "seats are almost gone" unless they are. Use actual numbers.
2. One CTA per post. Don't ask people to like, share, comment, AND register.
3. Rotate frameworks. Don't use the same concept twice in a row.
4. Match platform to content type. LinkedIn gets thought leadership. Instagram gets hooks and visuals. Facebook gets personal stories.
5. Every post must pass the voice check: Does this sound like Alex talking to a peer, or does it sound like a marketing department?
6. No em-dashes in any output. Use periods, commas, or line breaks instead.
7. No banned AI vocabulary (see brand-voice.md for full list).
8. Maximum one exclamation mark per post. Zero is better.
