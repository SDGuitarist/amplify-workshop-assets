## LAYER 2: THE FRAMEWORK

### Central Thesis: AI Dual Literacy

AI literacy alone builds demos. Domain expertise alone hits a ceiling. Together they build businesses.

This is the intellectual core of everything Alex does. It explains why a musician with zero coding background can ship production software: he brings 30 years of domain expertise (reading rooms, managing clients, running a business) and pairs it with AI systems that amplify that expertise rather than replacing it.

### Human-Led AI Philosophy

Human at the Start. AI in the Middle. Human at the End.

The human expert defines the problem, sets constraints, and makes final decisions. AI handles the middle: research, drafting, processing, pattern matching. The human validates, refines, and ships. This isn't a slogan. It's the operating principle behind every system Alex has built.

### Why He Has NOT Built End-to-End Automations

This is intentional. Alex's position: you diagnose first. You trust the system before letting it run autonomously. Every one of his pipelines has human checkpoints. The lead response system has quality gates. The grant consulting system has "never fabricate" as a structural constraint, not a suggestion. The workshop teaches this deliberately: build the judgment first, then the automation.

### Original Frameworks

**Decision and Evaluation:**
- **Three Questions** — Is this Human-Led? Does this use domain expertise? Does this build the business? Success criteria for any AI recommendation.
- **Six-Dimension Grant Evaluation** — Eligibility, Program Match, Award Size vs. Effort, Deadline Feasibility, Narrative Reuse, Strategic Value. Applied consistently across every opportunity.
- **Strategic Council Protocol** — Three-lens decision governance (Creative/Strategic, Skeptic, Operational) with Mode A (full council, irreversible decisions) and Mode B (light assessment, tactical decisions).
- **Feed-Forward Framework** — Three questions end every phase: hardest decision, rejected alternatives, least confident. Each phase's uncertainties become the next phase's priorities.
- **Plan Quality Gate** — No work starts unless the plan answers: What's changing? What must not change? How will we know it worked? Most likely way this plan is wrong?

**Teaching and Communication:**
- **AI Dual Literacy** — AI literacy + domain expertise = business capability.
- **The Wedge** — Work deeper, not faster. Makes competitor comparison irrelevant.
- **AI Anxiety to AI Agency** — Invisible emotional architecture in the workshop curriculum. Participants experience identity shift without it being named until the Integrate phase.
- **Expert-First Method** — Domain expertise drives the prompt, not the other way around.
- **5 I's** — Intent, Identity, Inference, Iteration, Integrity. Mnemonic for evaluating any AI interaction.
- **Slop Hook** — Pattern recognition for low-quality AI output. Gives people language for what they already sense.
- **The Mirror** — Self-reflection exercise at emotional peak, after intellectual stakes and before commitment.
- **Counter-Narrative (Floor and Ceiling)** — AI raising the floor makes the ceiling matter more. When anyone can generate, intentionality becomes premium.
- **"Capacity is the binding constraint, not knowledge"** — Reframe from "learn AI" to "deploy what you already know."
- **"Access not confidence"** — Positioning the workshop as closing an access gap, validated by Randstad research.

**Business Operations:**
- **Capacity-Realistic Consulting** — Every recommendation filtered through: can this specific person, with their specific bandwidth, actually execute this?
- **Four-File Client Knowledge Architecture** — Organization Profile, Programs/Narrative, Grant Landscape, Strategy/Actions. Each fact lives in one place. No duplication.
- **Narrative Clustering Strategy** — Group grants sharing content requirements so one application becomes source material for the next.
- **CONFIRMED vs. ESTIMATED Deadline Classification** — Binary label forcing honesty about deadline certainty.
- **Grant Readiness Roadmap** — New service tier for orgs not yet grant-ready. Turns honest "no" into a different engagement.
- **Cost Recovery Positioning** — Consulting fees as line items within grant applications. Reframes cost from expense to investment.
- **Sonic Terroir** — Extends the wine concept of terroir to entertainment programming. Every luxury property tells a cultural story; entertainment should complete it.
- **The Brand-Fit Premium** — Reframes live music from amenity to revenue lever, backed by published research.
- **Complement, Don't Replace** — Go-to-market strategy positioning PFE as specialty complement, not competitor replacement.
- **Experience Menu Model** — Venues select curated experiences rather than individual performers.
- **Captured Audience Framework** — Resort isolation reframed as F&B revenue opportunity.
- **Green/Yellow/Orange/Red Gig Protection Protocol** — Pain-indexed decision framework mapping chronic pain levels to specific performance actions.
- **Clinical Language Translation Method** — Systematic conversion of patient experience into clinician-ready language.

**Software Development (Created by Alex, Not the Plugin):**
- **Session-Kickoff Agent** — Custom subagent that reads HANDOFF.md, surfaces prior phase's "least confident" answer, sets scope. Prevents aimless exploration at session start.
- **Code-Explainer Agent** — Explains code through musician analogies with a persistent band story. Ends with ear training quizzes.
- **Pre-Commit-Check Agent** — GO/NO-GO gate on staged changes. Checks build, tests, focus, plan alignment, security, compound context.
- **Codex Integration Workflow** — Two AI systems (Claude Code + ChatGPT Codex) checking each other's work in a mandatory review sequence.
- **Promo-Post Command** — Custom command for voice-consistent social media content with mood auto-assignment and QA validation.
- **Mood Variant Design System** — 5 moods x 2 intensities for marketing graphics, mapped to a 48-post content calendar.
- **Workshop-Safe Constraint Set** — Minimum viable OS: 3 tiers, 2 modifiers, 1 safety flag, 1 QA checklist. Sophistication through restraint.
