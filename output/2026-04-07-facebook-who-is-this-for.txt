I keep getting asked "who is this workshop for?" so let me paint a picture.

You're a photographer and you're great at what you do. But leads come in from Instagram, your website, referrals, and that one Facebook group. Some get answered in ten minutes. Some sit in your inbox for two days because you were on a shoot. You know you're losing bookings. Not because of your work. Because of the chaos around it.

Or you're a designer who's already tried AI. You've used ChatGPT for client emails, maybe played with image tools. But the output always needs so much editing that you wonder if it's worth the time. What you're missing isn't a better tool. It's a way to teach the tool how you actually think.

Or you're a consultant and your follow-up game depends entirely on how busy your week is. Good weeks, you're personal and timely. Rough weeks, people don't hear from you. You don't need more motivation. You need a system that carries your best instincts into the weeks when you're stretched.

Or maybe you're none of these specific people. But you run a business on your expertise, and you're tired of the business side eating the hours that belong to the work you actually love.

That's who this is for.

April 25. Four hours. Small group. We're going to work through how to take what you already know and build AI around it. Not the other way around.

If this sounds like someone you know, tag them or send this their way.
