I taught an AI tool to write in my voice this week. It nailed the tone on the first try.

Then it quoted $1,950 to a family with an $800 budget. No alternative offered. No path forward. Just a price they couldn't afford and silence.

Voice without judgment is a well-written bad answer.

It took 17 rounds of testing to teach the judgment. When to adjust pricing. When to correct a genre request. When to compress a 160-word response down to 60 because there are 7 other vendors quoting and speed matters more than detail.

The voice took five minutes. The judgment took the real work.

That's what we build at the workshop. Not prompts. Judgment.
