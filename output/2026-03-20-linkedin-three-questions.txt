Where are you irreplaceable?

Not where are you good. Not where are you busy. Where does the whole thing fall apart if you're not in the room?

For me it's reading a client. A bride's mother calls and within two minutes I know whether she wants traditional boleros or something more contemporary. That's 30 years of reading rooms. No AI on the planet can do that.

Now the second question. Where are you just doing tasks?

For me it was drafting lead responses, following up with venues, keeping track of who said what and when. Important work. But it didn't require my judgment. It required my time. And my time was the bottleneck.

Third question. If you got those hours back, what would you actually do with them?

I got mine back. I built AI systems around the task work. Now I spend that time on music, on relationships, on the creative work that actually grows the business.

These three questions are how we start the workshop on April 25. Before anyone opens a laptop. Before we touch AI. Because the diagnosis has to come first.

What's your answer to question one?
