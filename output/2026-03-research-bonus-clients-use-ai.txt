64% of your potential clients use AI daily.

That number surprised me when I first saw it. Then I thought about what it actually means for anyone running a service-based business.

Your clients have seen AI output. They've used ChatGPT to draft their own emails. They've asked it to write social posts, summarize articles, plan trips. They know what generic sounds like because they've generated it themselves.

Which means when they get a response from you that reads like it could have come from anyone, they notice. Maybe not consciously. But the trust drops. The engagement drops. The reply rate drops.

And when they get something that's clearly from a person who knows their situation, who understands the nuance, who sounds like a real human with real experience? That stands out more than it ever has.

That's the shift I keep coming back to. AI didn't make expertise less valuable. It made expertise more visible. Because now there's a baseline that everyone can reach. The question is whether you're operating above it.
