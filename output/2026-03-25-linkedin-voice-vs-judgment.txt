My AI nailed my voice on the first try. Then it wrote a perfectly-worded wrong answer.

A client message that was too long for someone who needed a quick answer. Too casual for someone making a serious decision.

The words were mine but the instincts weren't.

It took 17 rounds of testing to teach the judgment. When to be brief and when someone needs the full picture. Which detail to cut and which one to keep because it changes everything.

The voice took five minutes. The judgment took the real work.

Voice without judgment is a well-written wrong answer.

Knowing what to say is one skill. Knowing what not to say is another. The second one is what makes your writing yours.

That's what we build at the workshop. Not prompts. Judgment.
